Edit a sharing operation

Guide utilisateur User guide Gebruikershandleiding Benutzerhandbuch

1 Open the sharing operations

Navigation
In the "Management" section, open "Members" then click "Sharing operations".

2 Open the sharing operation

Navigation
Click the row of the sharing operation you want to edit to open its page.

3 Operation detail

Editing an operation
The operation page shows its name, type, meters and covered municipalities. The pencils let you edit this information.

4 Open the edit form

Editing
Click "Edit" in the header — the form opens, pre-filled with the operation's current details.

5 Rename the operation

Editing an operation
Change the operation's name. You can also adjust its type and municipalities here.

6 Name updated

Confirmation
Back on the page, the operation carries its new name.

7 Edit the municipalities

Covered municipalities
The pencil next to the municipalities opens the editor for the operation's covered municipalities.

8 Add a municipality

Covered municipalities
Type a municipality and pick it from the suggestions to add it to the operation's scope.

9 Municipality added

Confirmation
The municipality "Ixelles" now appears among the operation's covered municipalities.
