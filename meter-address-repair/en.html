Fix meter addresses

Guide utilisateur User guide Gebruikershandleiding Benutzerhandbuch

1 Fix the addresses

Map coverage
The banner above the map says how many meters are not on it yet. Click "Fix the addresses" to open the queue of meters to reposition.

2 The repair queue

Meters to reposition
The dialog lists exactly the meters the map cannot place: those with no coordinates at all, and also those pinned only to the centre of their municipality.

3 Pick a meter

Selecting from the queue
Click a row to load its address into the form on the right. Repairs are made one meter at a time.

4 Address not found

Diagnosis
OptimCE queries the federal BeSt Address register. This address matches nothing there, which is why the meter is missing from the map.

5 Search for the right address

BeSt Address register
Type the first words of the real address. The suggestions come straight from the official Belgian address register.

6 Choose the suggestion

Selecting the address
Click the matching suggestion. The five detailed fields are filled with the register's official spelling.

7 Address located

Confirmation
The green message confirms the address is precisely located: the meter will appear on the map once the repair is saved.

8 Save and continue

Applying the repair
"Save and next" applies the fix and moves on to the next meter in the queue, without closing the dialog.

9 The map is up to date

Coverage after the repair
Back on the map, the banner has gone down: the repaired meter is now placed, and only the one pinned to its municipality centre remains.
