Send a procedure and follow its deadline

Send a ready procedure, see the regulator's delay start and find it on the deadline agenda.

Send a procedure and follow its deadline

1 Open the administrative documents

Navigation
In the sidebar, click "Administrative documents".

2 Ready to send

Procedures
Every row gives the next action and its due date without opening the procedure. Here every document is ready: the update is left to send before the date shown.

3 Send

Procedures
The main button offers the one action that matters now. The procedure being ready, it is "Send".

4 What to send

Send
OptimCE sends nothing itself. It prepares the recipient, the subject with its reference, a text to copy and the documents to attach; you send them from your mailbox or the regulator's online form.

5 Mark as sent

Send
Give the date and the channel, attach the email you sent if you like, then "Mark as sent". The procedure is then frozen: a change arriving later goes into a new procedure.

6 The regulator's delay is running

Deadlines
Sending starts the CWaPE's completeness-check delay, counted in Walloon working days. The next action becomes "Add a response": that is where you record its acknowledgement or its request for more information.

7 Every deadline

Administrative documents
The "Deadlines" tab gathers the delays of every procedure: yours as well as the regulator's or the grid operator's.

8 The deadline agenda

Deadlines
Every deadline says who has to act and by when; overdue ones rise to the top in red. It closes by itself when the expected response is recorded in the procedure.
