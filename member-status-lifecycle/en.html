Deactivate and reactivate a member

Deactivate a member then reactivate them, and follow the effect on their status and their meter.

For ManagerAdministrator

  • Step by step
  • Interactive
Step 1 of

Deactivate and reactivate a member

1 Open the members list

Navigation
In the "Management" section, open "Members" then click "Members".

2 Find the member

Member lifecycle
Search the member by name. Here we pick a member with no active meter, because a member tied to an active meter cannot be deactivated.

3 Open the member record

Navigation
Click the member's row in the table to open their record.

4 Active member record

Member lifecycle
The member is active: its status badge is green and a "Meter" tab is available. The action buttons let you deactivate it or put it on hold.

5 Deactivate the member

Member lifecycle
Click "Deactivate". The status change is immediate, with no extra confirmation.

6 Member deactivated

Member lifecycle
The badge switches to "Inactive" and the "Meter" tab disappears: an inactive member can no longer be tied to a meter.

7 Reactivate the member

Member lifecycle
The "Activate" button reactivates the member. You can manage a member's whole lifecycle from its record.

8 Member reactivated

Member lifecycle
The badge returns to "Active" and the "Meter" tab reappears. The member is fully operational again.
