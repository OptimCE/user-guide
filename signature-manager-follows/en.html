Follow the signature requests

See where each signature request of the community stands, remind a signer and read a request's history.

For ManagerAdministrator

Step 1 of

Follow the signature requests

1 Open the signatures

Navigation
In the sidebar, click "Signatures": every signature request of the community is gathered there.

2 The community's requests

Signatures
Every signature request is listed with its document, its origin, its progress, its deadline and its status. The filter at the top keeps only the requests of one status.

3 Where the request stands

Progress
The "Progress" counts the signatures received: here "0 of 1 signed", the participant has not signed yet.

4 Remind the signer

Reminder
Click "Send a reminder": every signer who has not signed yet gets a reminder by notification and by email.

5 Open the request

Signatures
Click "Open the details" to see the whole request.

6 The signers

Signature request
The top of the page gives the request's status and its deadline. Under "Signers", each one is listed with what they have done: invited, viewed, signed or declined.

7 The history

History
The "History" traces every step, the reminder included. "Log intact" confirms that nobody has changed it. The signed document only appears once everyone has signed.
