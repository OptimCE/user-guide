Add a meter to a sharing operation

Guide utilisateur User guide Gebruikershandleiding Benutzerhandbuch

1 Open my communities

Navigation
In the sidebar's profile menu, click "My communities" to see the list of communities you belong to.

2 Check the active community

Active community
Test Community is already your active one — its row offers "Information" and "Dashboard". If you belong to several communities, click "Activate" on the one you want to work in.

3 Open sharing operations

Navigation
Open the list of sharing operations for your community to find the one you want to attach a meter to.

4 Select an operation

Selection
Click the row of the sharing operation you want to edit to open its detail view.

5 Start adding a meter

Action
From the detail view, click "Add meter" to open the meter selection dialog.

6 Available meters

Selection
The dialog lists every meter in the community that is not yet attached to this sharing operation.

7 Tick the meter

Selection
Tick the checkbox of the meter you want to attach. Multiple meters can be selected before submitting.

8 Start date today

Activation window
Open the calendar and pick today's date so the meter attachment takes effect immediately.

9 Confirm the addition

Confirmation
Click "Validate" to attach the meter. A toast notification confirms the server-side update.

10 Meter attached

Verification
Back on the detail view, the newly attached meter appears immediately in the "Now" tab of meters tied to the operation.
