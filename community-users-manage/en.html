Manage the community's users

Guide utilisateur User guide Gebruikershandleiding Benutzerhandbuch

1 Open the users list

Navigation
In the sidebar's "Management" section, click "Users" to manage the people who have access to your community.

2 The community's users

Access management
This page lists everyone with access to the community and their role: administrator, manager or member. You can invite, change a role, or revoke access.

3 Invite a manager

Invitation
Click "Invite a manager" to email an invitation to someone who will help run the community.

4 Enter the email address

Invitation
Enter the email address of the person to invite. They will receive an invitation to join the community as a manager.

5 See pending invitations

Follow-up
The "See pending invitations" button opens the list of invitations that have been sent but not yet accepted, both members and managers.

6 Pending invitations

Follow-up
The "Managers" tab gathers invitations that have been sent but not yet accepted. While an invitation is pending, you can cancel it from this list.

7 Change a role

Roles
The pencil next to a user opens the role editor. You can promote a member to manager, or set a manager back to a plain member.

8 Save the role

Roles
Pick the new role from the list, then click "Save". The change takes effect immediately.

9 Role updated

Roles
The user is now a member: their role tag has changed and the remove action is now available on their row.

10 Remove a user

Removal
The red cross removes the user from the community right away — their access is revoked immediately, with no extra confirmation.

11 User removed

Confirmation
Searching for that email now returns nothing: the user no longer has access to the community.
