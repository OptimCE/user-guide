Follow sharing within an operation

Guide utilisateur User guide Gebruikershandleiding Benutzerhandbuch

1 Sharing within the operations

Dashboard
As soon as monitored meters belong to a sharing operation, the dashboard estimates, quarter-hour by quarter-hour, how much of the consumption the operation's production covered and how much of the injection was shared. It is an estimate among the monitored meters: the grid operator's data remains the reference.

2 Consumption drawn from the grid

Dashboard
This chart shows the energy the monitored meters drew from the grid. Solar production used on site is not included: the meters only see the exchange with the grid. It stays hidden while too few members contribute.

3 Choose a sharing operation

Scope
At the top of the dashboard, the "Scope" switches from the whole community to one of its sharing operations. Every figure and chart follows that choice.

4 The operation's figures

Sharing operation
For the chosen operation: its production over the last full hour, its exchanges with the grid, the shared energy (estimate) and how many of its meters are monitored. Grid exchanges and sharing stay hidden while the operation has too few members.
